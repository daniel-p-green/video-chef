# Proof and integration

A professional proof includes actual footage, actual intended copy, representative duration, entrance, hold, exit, relevant audio, and the intended crop. Review it at normal speed rather than as isolated stills.

Check subject visibility, hierarchy, text size, reading time, transition rhythm, color/contrast, caption collision, screen UI, compression, and whether the graphic feels like part of the film rather than a slide pasted over it.

Approval of one proof approves only the demonstrated direction. Scale it into an editable package after approval, then verify each integrated use in context. Keep graphics-only exports separate from integrated edit candidates.
