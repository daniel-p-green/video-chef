# Brief and preproduction checks

## Shared lock

Confirm audience, story promise, essential beats, runtime range, destination, aspect/orientation, tone, accessibility, evidence constraints, rights assumptions, owner, and definition of done.

## Interviews and documentary

Plan open questions, follow-ups, room tone, cutaways, names and pronunciations, consent, and factual verification. Do not script participant testimony or plan generated reenactments that could be mistaken for source footage.

## Product and screen demos

Map every spoken claim to a visible product state. Plan cursor movement, readable scale, private-data masking, failure-state coverage, and a clean fallback recording.

## Launch and social

Define whether the viewer arrives with sound on or off, where captions and UI chrome compete, the single action or takeaway, and which elements must survive alternate crops. Platform requirements can change; verify the intended destination at delivery time.

## Long form

Use chapters only when they represent real shifts in audience question or evidence. Preserve breathing room for important testimony and reduce repetition before shortening pauses that carry meaning.
