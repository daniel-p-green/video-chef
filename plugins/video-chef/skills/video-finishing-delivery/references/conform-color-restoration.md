# Conform, color, and restoration

Before finishing, relink full-resolution sources and verify source/sequence cadence, retimes, stabilization, transforms, proxies, nested comps, graphics, handles, alpha, field order, and audio sync.

Record the input and output color spaces, transfer characteristics, primaries, matrix, range, bit depth, and display assumptions when they matter. Tags support a color-managed chain but do not prove visual correctness; inspect on an appropriate display path.

For denoise, upscale, relight, deblur, reframing, beauty, or identity-affecting work, create matched source/candidate A/Bs at normal speed. Preserve texture, face/neck consistency, mouth/eyes/hair, motion, and temporal stability. Keep restoration separate from de-aging or beautification.

Reject a treatment that creates flicker, halos, plastic texture, age shift, temporal warping, color discontinuity, or worse performance perception, even if sharpness metrics improve.
