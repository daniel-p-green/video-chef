# Edit grammar

## Dialogue and interviews

Build for meaning and performance. Use J/L cuts and cutaways to smooth necessary edits, not to conceal fabricated statements. Preserve pauses that show thought or feeling when they serve the story.

## Multicam

Switch for speaker, reaction, visual information, or purposeful energy. Maintain eyelines and action direction. Avoid automatic switching that creates nervous visual churn.

## Demos

Show the product state that supports the words. Slow down around consequential actions; omit cursor wandering and dead waits. Do not imply a step succeeded if the screen recording does not show it.

## Short form

Earn the opening quickly, but do not confuse speed with clarity. Design for likely sound-off viewing and interface overlays. Keep one main takeaway.

## Long form

Use changes in audience question, evidence, location, or emotional state to structure chapters. Remove redundant explanations before compressing every breath.
