# Timeline and lineage

Record the canonical project, parent sequence or composition, candidate name, creation time, source map version, brief version, editor/automation, and intended review purpose.

Use a new sequence or isolated project copy for material experiments. Never overwrite the only editable master, flatten source relationships prematurely, or infer authority from a filename containing `final`.

Before editing, verify frame rate/timebase, dimensions, audio layout, source start timecode, sync method, proxies, nests/precomps, linked graphics, and offline media. After editing, reopen or re-query the result and verify the timeline contains the expected ranges and assets.

Keep decisions and mechanical changes separate: a decision log explains why; a change log records what changed. Use `../../assets/DECISION_LOG.template.md` and `../../assets/CHANGE_LOG.template.md`.
