# Captions and accessible media

W3C describes captions as synchronized text for spoken words, necessary speaker identity, and important sounds. Automatically generated captions generally need editing. Source: https://www.w3.org/WAI/perspective-videos/captions/

Check verbatim accuracy, names, terminology, punctuation, speaker changes, sound cues, synchronization, duration, reading speed, line length, line count, orphaned words, shot changes, and collision with essential graphics. Caption placement should not hide faces, product controls, or source evidence.

Use SRT for broad interchange and WebVTT when the destination needs WebVTT features. Preserve a clean master transcript separately from timed captions.

WCAG time-based-media requirements vary by media type and conformance level. Important visual information may require a descriptive transcript or audio description in addition to captions. Check the actual delivery context against current W3C guidance: https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/time-based-media.html and https://www.w3.org/WAI/media/av/description/

Do not treat automated timing metrics as final accessibility approval. Review the captions over the real picture at normal speed.
